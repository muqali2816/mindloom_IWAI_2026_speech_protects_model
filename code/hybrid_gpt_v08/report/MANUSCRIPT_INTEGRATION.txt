Integration checklist for the next manuscript revision

1. Introduction
Start from Friston & Frith (2015) and Friston, Parr et al. (2020), then pose the
specific identification question. Do not say all prior active-inference accounts
assume cooperation or guarantee learning. Do not equate agreement with learning.

2. Part I
Keep the empirical engine evaluation and its own benchmark definition. Keep
primary versus alternate labels and the SHIFT event distinction. The hybrid
has not rerun an LLM recognizer or produced new speech-annotation accuracy.
Do not recast all 169 historical examples as Tier-1 feature evidence. Explain
how the new two-form measurement would be annotated prospectively.

3. Formal bridge
Use the explicit form-conditioned label channel and its uncertainty. Explain
that archived LOCK/SEEK rows are provisional anchors, not validated measurement
of invitation/restriction. The full ontology is not itself simulated.

4. Part II
Add observed/inferred/assumed table and exact timing. If replacing the earlier
single-agent or M-bridge model with v0.8, replace its entire methods and result
block consistently. Do not mix a v0.8 row into the old recovery table.
The code changes action names, timing, private data and observer priors.

5. Results
Separate belief accuracy, belief change, public switching and verification
arrival. State the public-persistence/private-updating counterexample explicitly.
Report beta=0: the access-protection effect does not require information value.
Report arrival prediction, next-act prediction and mechanism recovery separately.
Report the approximation boundary of the long hidden-form observer.

6. Discussion and abstract
Claim a computable in-silico observation bridge and testable interaction process.
Do not claim uniquely active-inference behavior, inferred human motives, detected
allostatic burden, clinical validation or full generative-model learning.

7. Reviewer issues not resolved by this model
Benchmark construction, independence of annotation, held-out definitions,
baseline engine comparisons, high-tier annotation reliability, underlying LLM
reproducibility and physiological validation still need their own evidence.
A formal model cannot substitute for these empirical checks.

8. Poster
Use one observation loop, three mechanism descriptions, one mixed-pair result,
and one prediction-vs-identification comparison. Keep the untested physiology
in a roadmap box. All v0.8 values must be labelled synthetic/exploratory.
